## Journey models and imagery

The home page journey uses files in `public/journey/`:

- `iss.glb` (International Space Station (B)) and `voyager.glb` (Voyager Probe (A)) from NASA 3D Resources, https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources, used under NASA's media usage guidelines. NASA does not endorse this project.
- `earth_*.jpg/png` and `moon_1024.jpg`: the planet textures from the three.js examples (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js, MIT), made from NASA imagery.
- `public/draco/`: the Draco decoder shipped with three.js (Apache-2.0), used to decompress the models.

The Sun, the galaxy, the Big Bang and the journey's sound design are generated in code.
